For integrators

We engineer it. You install it.

DAVE is looking for AV integrators worldwide to deliver our projects locally. We bring the design, the drawings and the AI tooling. You bring your team, your local knowledge and your installation quality.

What DAVE provides

  • Full design package. Drawings, schematics, rack layouts, cable schedules and a bill of materials.
  • AI project management. Programme, procurement and progress run on one AI-driven platform you can see.
  • Pre-built in the UK. Racks arrive assembled, wired and tested, ready to install.
  • AI simulation. Acoustic, sightline and coverage modelling signed off before install.
  • Programming files. Control, DSP and network configurations ready to load.
  • Remote commissioning. Our engineers test and tune with your team from Dubai.
  • Ongoing support. Monitoring and a direct line to the design team after handover.

What partners provide

  • Local installation. Certified technicians and project management on site.
  • Site knowledge. Regulations, access and trades coordination in your market.
  • Logistics. Receiving, storage and staging of equipment.
  • First-line service. Hands on site when something needs attention.

Integrator FAQ

Questions partners ask

How working with DAVE fits around your business.

Where do you need integration partners?

Everywhere our clients are. We are especially keen to hear from integrators in Europe, the GCC, Africa, India and Asia Pacific.

Who contracts with the end client?

It depends on the project. Sometimes DAVE holds the contract and engages you to install, and sometimes you hold it and engage DAVE for design and commissioning. We agree this before every job.

Do we need to use specific brands?

We design around what suits the client, usually with our technology partners. If you have strong local supply relationships, tell us, as that helps with lead times and support.

How does remote commissioning work?

Your team connects the system to a secure remote access point. Our engineers in Dubai then run automated tests, load configurations and tune the system with you on a call.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. Partners are paid for the work they deliver on each project.
