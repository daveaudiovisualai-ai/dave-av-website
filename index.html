Our approach · brief to handover

AI at every stage of the signal chain

Every project follows the same six stages. At each one, AI tools take on the repetitive and computational work, and an experienced consultant signs off the result.

  1. 01 · Survey

    Site capture

    Photos, LiDAR scans and drawings become an accurate 3D model of the room, often without a site visit.

    AI: photogrammetry and scan-to-model
  2. 02 · Simulate

    Acoustics and sightlines

    We model reverberation, speech intelligibility and viewing angles before a single product is chosen.

    AI: acoustic prediction, STI and RT60 modelling
  3. 03 · Design

    System design

    Schematics, rack layouts, cable schedules and bills of materials are drafted automatically and checked by an engineer.

    AI: drawing generation and spec checking
  4. 04 · Procure

    Global sourcing

    We compare pricing, lead times and regional availability across manufacturers and distributors in every market we work in.

    AI: price and lead-time analysis
  5. 05 · Commission

    Testing and tuning

    Automated test scripts verify every input, output and control path. DSP is tuned against measured room data.

    AI: automated test runs and EQ tuning
  6. 06 · Support

    Monitoring

    After handover, systems report their health to us. We spot failing lamps, drives and links before users do.

    AI: predictive maintenance alerts
